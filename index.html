Each symbol uses the last 55 or more closed 1-minute Binance candles. EMA(9) vs EMA(21), RSI(14), and latest candle direction each contribute one vote. A coin shows UP when at least two indicators favor UP, DOWN when at least two favor DOWN, otherwise WAIT. The combined signal needs at least 7 of 10 coins to agree; otherwise WAIT. This is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability or a Binomo signal.
