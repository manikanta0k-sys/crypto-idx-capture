iPhone screenshot shortcut setup (optional)

In Binomo, take a screenshot → tap Share → choose Binomo to Dashboard. Shortcut saves the image to Photos and opens this dashboard. Then tap the screenshot upload area, select the newest photo, and Analyze. iOS cannot silently send Binomo app screenshots to this website.

  1. Open Apple Shortcuts → + → New Shortcut. Name it Binomo to Dashboard.
  2. Tap shortcut settings → Show in Share Sheet → accept Images.
  3. Add action Save to Photo Album; input: Shortcut Input.
  4. Add action URL; enter your published GitHub Pages dashboard address.
  5. Add action Open URLs; input: URL.
  6. In Binomo, take screenshot → Photos → Share → Binomo to Dashboard. In Chrome, tap screenshot selector and choose latest photo.
Clipboard image paste may not be available in iPhone Chrome. Photo picker always remains available.
