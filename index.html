03 / Methodology

  • Automatic full-chart detection excludes the bottom trade buttons and top account controls.
  • Identify bright red/green candle pixels, group adjacent x-columns and estimate recent candle direction.
  • Ignore the rightmost candle for directional counts because it may still be forming.
  • Never claim exact RSI, MACD, Supertrend, ADX, OHLC, or 80% accuracy from image colors alone.
  • For reliable expiry testing, collect timestamped OHLC series and real outcome labels; screenshots alone cannot establish forward returns.
